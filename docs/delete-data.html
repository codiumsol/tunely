Tunely Radio – Delete your data

App: Tunely: Live AM FM World Radio (Tunely Radio) by codiumsol · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Tunely Radio has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The little data it sends is anonymous and linked only to a random install number. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Tunely Radio and go to Settings.
  2. Tap the version number at the bottom 5 times. Your install number appears; tap Copy.
  3. Email raabtify@gmail.com with the subject "Tunely Radio data deletion" and paste your install number. If you also want a report or station suggestion removed, tell us the station and roughly when you sent it.

We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you can no longer open the app, email us anyway and describe what you want removed.

What is deleted

  • Daily usage numbers for your install number (days used, minutes listened, times opened, ads shown and clicked).
  • Reports and station suggestions you identify, including any note you typed.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Totals that are already added up with other users (for example "ads shown in a country on a day") contain nothing about you and are kept.
  • Without a request, daily usage numbers are deleted automatically after 120 days, and the scrambled (hashed) internet address used against spam after 30 days.
  • Your favourites, history and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Purchases are kept by Google Play under your Google account; we never hold payment details.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob for ads is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings and manage it at My Ad Center.
