Tunely Radio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Tunely Radio ("the app") lets you listen to live radio stations from your own country and around the world. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

No account, no personal details

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

Anonymous usage numbers

To keep the app working well and to decide how many ads to show, the app sends our server (run on Cloudflare) a few anonymous numbers: a random install number created by the app (not linked to your name, phone number, account or advertising ID; reinstalling the app creates a new one), your chosen home country, the app version, how many minutes you listened and how many times you opened the app each day, and how many ads were shown and clicked and what they earned. These numbers are used only to improve the app and its ads, are never sold, and daily activity is deleted after 120 days.

Information kept on your phone

Your favorite stations, recently played stations, language choice and ad-free status are saved only on your own device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Radio streams

The app does not host any audio. When you play a station, your phone connects directly to that radio station's own streaming server, which can see your IP address like any website you visit. Station listings come from the public Radio Browser directory and from the stations' own official websites (for example Radio Pakistan).

Reports and suggestions

When you use Help & report (for example "Won't play" or "Suggest a station") the app sends to our server, run on Cloudflare: the problem type you chose, the station involved (its name, id, stream address and country), the note you typed, the time it happened if you leave that switched on, and the app version, app language, your chosen home country and your phone's system version. It never sends your name, account, contacts or location. To stop spam, the server keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address for 30 days and then deletes it. Reports are used only to fix and add stations. If you are offline, the report waits on your phone and is sent later; unsent reports are deleted after 7 days. To have a report removed, email us (below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads, and in some regions to personalize them. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional yearly plan and the one-time "Remove ads forever" purchase are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app only which of these your Google account owns. The yearly plan renews until you cancel it in Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Copyright

All station names, logos and broadcasts belong to their owners. If you own a station and want it removed or corrected, contact us and we will act promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
